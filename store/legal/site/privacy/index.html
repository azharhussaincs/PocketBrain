PocketBrain Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30 · App package: com.pocketbrain.app · Contact: support@pocketbrain.app

1. Overview

PocketBrain is an offline-first AI operating system for mobile devices. Chats, documents, images, audio, and downloaded models stay on your device by default. PocketBrain does not require an account and does not operate a mandatory cloud AI backend for core features.

2. Data processed on your device

  • AI prompts, chat history, Workspace documents, generated files, and model files stored locally
  • Device hardware information used only to recommend compatible models
  • Optional speech audio processed by on-device / OS speech engines when you use Speech features
  • Camera or photo-library images you explicitly select for Vision / OCR / Image features
  • App settings and consent choices stored in local app storage

3. Data we do not collect by default

  • No advertising identifiers for ads (no ads SDK is included at launch)
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK bundled in the app binary at launch
  • No automatic upload of chats, documents, images, or audio to PocketBrain servers
  • No sale of personal information

4. Network use

Internet access is used when you choose to download open-source models from third-party hosts (for example model CDNs), open external links, or share exported files through the system share sheet. Model downloads transfer model weight files — not your chat history — to or from those hosts over HTTPS when you start a download.

5. Permissions

Microphone, camera, and media library permissions are requested only when you start a feature that needs them. You can revoke permissions in system settings at any time. Denying a permission disables the related feature without blocking the rest of the app.

6. Third-party models and licenses

Downloaded models remain subject to their authors’ licenses. Review each model’s license in the Marketplace before download.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in-app (models, files, chats) or clear app storage / uninstall. PocketBrain does not retain a cloud copy of your chats.

8. Children

PocketBrain is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: support@pocketbrain.app
