PocketBrain AI Disclaimer

Last updated: 2026-07-30

On-device models

PocketBrain runs open-source models you download to your device. Model quality, speed, and language support vary by model size, quantization, and your hardware.

Outputs can be wrong

  • Outputs can be incorrect, biased, outdated, or fabricated
  • Do not treat outputs as professional advice (legal, medical, financial, or otherwise)
  • Always review exports and shared content before sending them to others

Capability honesty

PocketBrain does not invent AI text, OCR text, vision answers, or image pixels when a required model or native runtime is missing. Some features remain gated until a compatible runtime is available.

You are responsible for how you use outputs and for complying with each model’s license and applicable law.
