PocketBrain FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. PocketBrain works without accounts.

Does PocketBrain send my chats to the cloud?

No. Chats and documents stay on your device by default. Internet is used when you download models or open links you choose.

Why download a model?

On-device AI needs local model weights. You can install several models, switch anytime, and delete any model to free storage.

Will Expo Go run real GGUF models?

No. Real GGUF inference needs a native build with llama.rn. Expo Go uses a clearly labeled development mock.
